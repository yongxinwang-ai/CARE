# Task 001: Primal-Dual Budgeter for Adaptive Multimodal Reasoning

## High-Level Specification

Implement a Primal-Dual Budgeter (PDB) for adaptive multimodal reasoning that unifies direct answer, textual CoT, and visual CoT with optimal stopping under dual budgets (visual and text).

### Key Features
1. **Dual Budget Optimal Stopping**: Simultaneously constrain visual budget (visual tokens/cropping/drawing) and text budget (CoT/answer tokens)
2. **Action-level Gain/Cost Learning**: Provide lightweight estimation heads for information gain and dual costs
3. **Integration with Ground-R1**: Maintain two-stage rollouts + group-normalized GRPO, modifying rewards to "accuracy - dual costs"
4. **Adaptive λ Control**: Dual variable updates to meet target budgets
